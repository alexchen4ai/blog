I'm **Alex (Wei) Chen**. I work on AI systems end to end — from model research to the generative AI inference stacks that run those models on real hardware.

I'm a **Director** at Qualcomm, where I lead generative AI inference research and engineering for LLMs and VLMs on **Snapdragon** — especially the **Hexagon NPU** — spanning hardware-aware quantization, operator fusion, memory scheduling, and kernel tuning.

I **founded** [Nexa AI](https://nexa.ai) and served as its **Founder, CEO, and Chief Scientist** until Qualcomm acquired it in 2026. We built [NexaSDK](https://github.com/NexaAI/nexa-sdk), an open-source inference runtime for NPU, GPU, and CPU with **8,000+ GitHub stars**. I was the main technical author of the **Octopus** model series, **OmniVLM**, **OmniAudio**, and **NexaQuant**, worked with engineering teams at [NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/rtx-ai-garage-nexa-hyperlink-local-agent/), [AMD](https://www.amd.com/en/developer/resources/technical-articles/2025/advancing-ai-with-nexa-ai--image-generation-on-amd-npu-with-sdxl.html), [Microsoft](https://blogs.windows.com/windowsexperience/2025/11/18/ignite-2025-windows-at-the-frontier-of-work/), [Google](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/gemma-family-and-toolkit-expansion-io-2024/), [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/new/announcements/ibm-granite-4-0-hyper-efficient-high-performance-hybrid-models), and [Intel](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/intel-software_ai-ondeviceai-nexasdk-activity-7376337062087667712-xw7i), and deployed with enterprise partners like **Geely**, **HP**, **Lenovo**, and **İşbank**.

My interests sit at the intersection of **AI systems**, **generative AI inference**, **hardware-software co-design**, and **robotics** — especially how we build intelligence that can act in the physical world. I hold a PhD from **Stanford University** (2024).
